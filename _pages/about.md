As Assistant Professor in the Department of Information Management at [Chaoyang University of Technology](https://www.cyut.edu.tw/) (CYUT), Taichung, Taiwan, I have taught and conducted research since 2019. Trained at the [Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur](https://www.iitk.ac.in/), where I earned both a PhD and an M.Tech in Industrial and Management Engineering, I joined CYUT as a postdoctoral fellow in technological innovation and patent analysis before moving to the faculty.

My research lies at the intersection of **quality management, operations and digital technology**. It examines how firms implement Total Quality Management and its successors, Quality 4.0 and Quality 5.0; how Industry 4.0 and 5.0 technologies reshape supply-chain performance and resilience; and how patent citation networks can map technological trajectories and forecast convergence. A growing body of this work addresses the circular economy, green human resource management and sustainable operations in emerging economies.
