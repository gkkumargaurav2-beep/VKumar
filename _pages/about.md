I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Information Management at [Chaoyang University of Technology](https://www.cyut.edu.tw/) (CYUT), Taichung, Taiwan, where I have taught since 2019. I hold a PhD and an M.Tech in Industrial and Management Engineering from the [Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur](https://www.iitk.ac.in/), and I was a postdoctoral fellow in technological innovation and patent analysis at CYUT before joining the faculty.

My research sits where **quality management, operations and digital technology** meet. I study how firms implement Total Quality Management and its successors (Quality 4.0 and Quality 5.0), how Industry 4.0 and 5.0 technologies reshape supply-chain performance and resilience, and how patent citation networks can be used to map technological trajectories and forecast convergence. A growing strand of my work examines the circular economy, green HRM and sustainable operations in emerging economies.
